# Stickalo — Support

[Español](#soporte) · [Privacy Policy](privacy) · [Terms of Use](terms)

Stickalo keeps your stickers in one place: rescue them from your WhatsApp chats, make new ones from photos and videos, and send them to WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram and any other app.

**Contact:** [hello@marloncoreas.com](mailto:hello@marloncoreas.com). Tell us your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**How do I rescue the stickers from a WhatsApp chat?**
In WhatsApp open the chat, tap its name, then *Export chat › Attach media*. In the share sheet choose **Stickalo**. Open Stickalo and your stickers will be there. Only the stickers are imported; messages, photos and voice notes are never opened.

**How do I send a pack to WhatsApp?**
Open the pack, tap **Send** and choose WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens and asks you to add the pack. WhatsApp takes animated and static stickers in separate packs of up to 30, so Stickalo splits large packs for you.

**How do I use my stickers in iMessage?**
In Messages, open the apps row next to the text field and choose Stickalo.

**How do I turn on the keyboard?** (Pro)
Go to *Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Stickalo*, then tap Stickalo and turn on *Allow Full Access*. Full Access is needed only to copy the sticker so you can paste it. The keyboard does not record what you type.

**"Create with AI" doesn't appear.**
It uses Image Playground, which is available only on devices with Apple Intelligence.

**I bought Pro and it isn't active.**
Open *Account › Restore purchases*. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
In *Account › Manage subscription*, or in iOS *Settings › your name › Subscriptions*.

**Where are my stickers stored?**
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Preguntas frecuentes

**¿Cómo rescato los stickers de un chat de WhatsApp?**
En WhatsApp abre el chat, toca su nombre y luego *Exportar chat › Adjuntar multimedia*. En el menú Compartir elige **Stickalo**. Abre Stickalo y tus stickers estarán ahí. Solo se importan los stickers; los mensajes, fotos y notas de voz nunca se abren.

**¿Cómo envío un pack a WhatsApp?**
Abre el pack, toca **Enviar** y elige WhatsApp. WhatsApp se abre y te pide agregar el pack. WhatsApp recibe los stickers animados y los estáticos en packs separados de hasta 30, así que Stickalo divide los packs grandes por ti.

**¿Cómo uso mis stickers en iMessage?**
En Mensajes, abre la fila de apps junto al campo de texto y elige Stickalo.

**¿Cómo activo el teclado?** (Pro)
Ve a *Ajustes › General › Teclado › Teclados › Añadir nuevo teclado › Stickalo*, luego toca Stickalo y activa *Permitir acceso total*. El acceso total solo se necesita para copiar el sticker y que puedas pegarlo. El teclado no registra lo que escribes.

**No aparece "Crear con IA".**
Usa Image Playground, que solo está disponible en dispositivos con Apple Intelligence.

**Compré Pro y no está activo.**
Abre *Cuenta › Restaurar compras*. Revisa que tengas iniciada la sesión con la cuenta de Apple con la que compraste.

**¿Cómo cancelo mi suscripción?**
En *Cuenta › Administrar suscripción*, o en *Ajustes › tu nombre › Suscripciones* de iOS.

**¿Dónde se guardan mis stickers?**
Solo en tu dispositivo. Si eliminas la app, se borran.
